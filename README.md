# ProteinPulse

ProteinPulse is a free calorie and protein tracker that runs in your web browser. You set a daily goal, type in the numbers you already know as you eat, and see how much room is left for the day.

**Live site:** https://azqato.github.io/protein/

## What it offers

- **Today:** two progress rings show calories and protein against your goal, with how much is left and a list of what you logged.
- **Quick logging:** tap the + button, enter calories and protein (a food name is optional), and you are done. Foods you logged before can be refilled with one tap.
- **History:** week, month, and year charts compare each day with your goal, plus averages and how often you hit your protein target.
- **Goal:** set a calorie and protein goal once; it carries forward every day until you change it.
- **Your data, your file:** everything stays in your browser. You can export it to a spreadsheet at any time and import it back on another device.

It works on a phone like an app and on a desktop in a phone-sized frame or fullscreen. There is no account, no food database to search, and no server that sees what you eat.

## Who it is for

People who track calories and protein for a cut, bulk, or general fitness goal, already know their numbers from food labels or a coach, and want a fast, private place to log them.

## Status

Live and in active development. It is free and open source.
